# Dispatch-and-wait liveness — arm a turn-firer, then bilateral self-checks

**Status:** Adopted 2026-06-26 by Orchestrator (HO) — operationalizes [`architecture/adr/ORG-037-team-coordination-liveness.md`](../../adr/ORG-037-team-coordination-liveness.md) §Layer 1 + §Layer 2 + the process lesson, which the operator ratified 2026-06-26 (T-1322: "layers 1-2 yes"). Below-goal-level operational SOP landing on that ratification per ORG-037 §Implementation-arc A; HO-authored per ORG-012. The operator override-available. Layer 3 (the narrow Recovery-side backstop) is operator-gated + Recovery-designed and is NOT in this SOP.

## When to use

Every time you dispatch a teammate (project-orchestrator, implementer, reviewer, researcher) and enter an **await-state** — you're waiting on their result to proceed. Applies at every dispatcher↔dispatchee boundary: HO↔orchestrator, orchestrator↔implementer, orchestrator↔reviewer, HO↔researcher.

Not used: fire-and-forget dispatches where you do not block on the result (rare — most dispatches are await-states).

## Why — the load-bearing finding

Every discipline-tier self-check (the dispatcher's active-team worker-silence smell-test, the dispatchee's pending-action self-check) runs **only at end-of-turn — only when a turn fires.** The dominant failure mode is *precisely that the wake which would fire the turn dropped*: the team wake-hook is intermittently unreliable — worker→dispatcher and CI-completion wakes drop; `bugs/team-pattern-sendmessage-intermittent-wake-failure.md`. Therefore **no arrangement of end-of-turn checks can close the gap** — a turn-firer *independent of the flaky wake* is structurally required.

Motivating incident (`incidents/2026-06-25-orchestrator-stall-dropped-wake/`): a reviewer APPROVE-wake routed to the orchestrator and dropped; the orchestrator never resumed and nothing re-invoked HO either, so HO's smell-test never ran. ~4h zero progress, surfaced only by the operator's unrelated "how are things?". Every such surfacing is a withdrawal on D1.

## Layer 1 — arm a self-wakeup when you dispatch-and-wait (load-bearing)

The turn-firer. Do this as part of the dispatch ritual:

1. **Arm on dispatch.** The moment you dispatch a teammate and will wait on the result, ARM a periodic self-wakeup. At the HO/session tier this is the `ScheduleWakeup` harness primitive — independent of the team wake-hook, so it fires the turn the discipline checks need.
2. **Cadence — bounded, calibrated to what you're waiting for** (the ORG-037 §Forces D10/P6 tension — catch-fast vs burn-budget):
   - **Idle fallback heartbeat: ≥1200s** when no specific near-term event is expected (just guarding against a dropped wake). Don't burn budget on no-op cycles.
   - **Active poll: ~270s** (inside the 5-minute prompt-cache window) ONLY when a specific event is imminent — a plan-report due, CI nearing completion, a merge-completion you must catch.
   - **Never ~300s** — worst-of-both (pays the cache miss without amortizing it). Drop to ~270s to stay in cache, or commit to ≥1200s.
3. **Ground-truth on each fire.** Don't trust the wake; verify actual arc progress: dispatchee transcript/inbox mtime (`~/.claude/teams/*/inboxes/`, the subagent transcript jsonl) + repo / PR / branch / ticket state vs expected. Then decide: await resolved → proceed; else re-poke via the **reliable** direction (below) + re-arm.
   - **Build-heavy dispatchees — `ps`, not mtime (T-1316 lesson).** A long `cargo`/`rustc`/`npm`/`pytest` subprocess **freezes the transcript mtime at command-issue time** for its whole duration — which reads as "parked" and provokes a premature poke or stand-down (the whipsaw §poke→verify→decide warns against). Before concluding a stall on a build-heavy worker, check `ps -eo pid,etimes,comm | grep -E 'cargo|rustc|clippy-driver|node|pytest'`: active build processes = alive, not stalled. (In the T-1316 arc, transcript-mtime read "idle ~4 min" while the implementer was mid-`cargo test`; `ps` showed it building. A second tell: many concurrent build invocations contending on one `target/` lock = *two* dispatchees on one worktree, not one slow one.)
4. **Re-arm until the await resolves.** Disarm when no in-flight dispatch remains (no stray heartbeats).

**Why this is NOT the rejected ORG-011 Path-3.** ORG-011 rejected `ScheduleWakeup` as the primary *HO-drift* mechanism because it "depends on HO remembering every turn" and HO-drift has no trigger moment. The dispatch-and-wait trigger is **single and natural** — *arm-when-you-dispatch-and-wait*, bound to the dispatch ritual, self-re-arming thereafter. One action at one well-defined moment, not a per-turn discipline.

## Layer 2 — bilateral self-checks (defense-in-depth; runs when a turn fires)

- **Dispatcher half:** on every turn while holding an in-flight dispatch, ground-truth the dispatchee against its **role-threshold** — orchestrator 30 min / implementer 15 min / reviewer 5 min — and if stale AND this turn wasn't you actively messaging it, status-check via the **reliable direction**: spawner→subagent `SendMessage` (HO→worker from outside the team) or the harness **task-notification-to-spawner**. NEVER rely on the flaky member→member wake.
- **Dispatchee half:** on every end-of-turn, self-check *"do I hold a completed-but-un-acted-on result in my arc?"* — APPROVE'd-but-unmerged, done-but-unreported, reviewed-but-unforwarded — and act on it rather than waiting for a wake to re-prompt.

**Necessary but not sufficient alone:** Layer 2 still runs only inside a turn — it depends on Layer 1 (or a normal wake) to fire that turn. Either half breaks a given stall; Layer 1 + both halves is the robust posture.

## The process lesson: poke → verify-liveness → decide

On a *suspected-dead* dispatchee: ground-truth its **actual liveness** (transcript/inbox mtime + repo/PR state) immediately after the wake poke and **before** either taking over or issuing contradictory instructions. No speculative parallel takeover. (The incident recovery whipsawed — wake → "belay, I'll take it" → "disregard belay" — creating momentary double-action; harmless only because the duplicate push was idempotent. Don't rely on idempotence.)

**The terminal case — presumed-dead disposition (added 2026-07-19, T-1791; [`bugs/fanout-worker-wedge-nudge-nonresponsive-no-task-visibility.md`](../../bugs/fanout-worker-wedge-nudge-nonresponsive-no-task-visibility.md)).** Both prongs above assume the worker either answers a nudge or leaves a pollable surface. One observed wedge shape (rsch-aws, T-1787 fan-out) defeats both: repeated `idle_notification` without a report, **two** direct nudges ignored entirely (vs the sibling idle-without-report bug's 11/11 nudge-recovery record), and `TaskOutput`/`TaskList` both blind ("No task found"). On that **triple signature** — after one nudge given the 2–3 min idle/message-race grace plus one explicit "send partial or declare nothing" ultimatum — stop waiting: **presume dead, close the lane honestly as UNVERIFIED (a dead lane's absence must never read as coverage), and re-dispatch a fresh agent.** Third-prong salvage before respawn where warranted: the spawned agent's transcript JSONL under `~/.claude/projects/<cwd-slug>/` persists even when the task registry is blind — read-only inspection tells you whether work product exists worth carrying into the fresh brief. Do not burn additional nudges; nudge-recovery is the *sibling* bug's signature, and its absence is what distinguishes this one.

## Field evidence (validated live)

- **2026-08-12 T-2652/T-2909 broker arc:** the documented intermediate-tier routing shape hit 2× in one dispatch (a PO's implementer completed its plan twice — original + verbatim re-send — and both completions surfaced as task-notifications at the session root, never waking the PO; HO→PO directed sends stayed 7/7). Two recovery refinements validated: (a) **transcript-extraction beats re-request even outside the presumed-dead case** — the PO read its child's final assistant message straight from the on-disk JSONL (bounded line-parser, last assistant text block) and got the deliverable verbatim with zero re-derivation and zero reconstruction risk; use this as the ROUTINE recovery for a completed-but-undelivered child report, reserving re-request for when the transcript is genuinely absent; (b) **session-root relay-watchdog** — since the root SEES the orphaned completions, HO armed a harness-tracked background mtime-watch on the PO inbox (activity ⇒ exit 0 / N-min silence ⇒ exit 1; either exit re-invokes HO) and relay-nudged on the silence branch — the Layer-1 turn-firer expressed as a cheap file-watch when the wait target is a specific peer's next action.

- **Dispatcher-watchdog caught dropped wakes at 3+ consecutive arc transitions** in one Echo Phase-3 merge arc (B1-APPROVE→orchestrator, A1b-APPROVE→orchestrator, A1b-merge-completion→orchestrator) — each caught in seconds via Layer-1 self-wakeup + ground-truth + poke-from-outside-team, vs the ~4h opening stall. The recurrence count (3 in one arc) is the empirical case for the structural mechanism over prose.
- **CI→merge wake-gap:** prefer GitHub-native auto-merge where the repo enables `enablePullRequestAutoMerge`; else **single-process poll-then-merge** — poll CI (`gh run view --json status`, NOT `gh run watch`) in one continuous background run and, on green, `gh pr merge --admin` in the SAME process, never yielding a turn between CI-green and merge so there's no inter-turn wake to drop. Residual: neither bridges **merge-completion→next-step** — that transition stays a watchdog point.
- **Reliable signal direction (T-1320 — RESOLVED 2026-06-26).** Reliability is **DIRECTIONAL**, not "SendMessage vs notification." The single flaky primitive is the **team wake-hook that re-activates an IDLE PEER on an inbound member→member `SendMessage`.** Two directions are RELIABLE (zero drops across this session's Agent dispatches + the bug pin): **child stops → parent gets task-notification / tool-return** (Agent-subagent completion is rock-solid), and **parent → child `SendMessage`** (spawner→worker, outside-team direction). **Tier caveat (`bugs/teammate-orchestrator-agent-dispatch-completion-routes-to-main.md`):** the "child→parent completion reliable" claim was validated at the **top tier** (T-1320, Recovery's own Agent subagents, where spawner==session-root). At the **intermediate-teammate tier** (a teammate-orchestrator's Agent-children, spawner≠session-root), the completion routes to the **session-root** (HO), NOT the intermediate spawner — so an intermediate orchestrator does not reliably receive its own children's completions. This is the structural reason HO drives completion-driven arcs directly (ORG-036), rather than nesting a teammate-orchestrator that must loop on its own children. **Dispatch discipline that follows (in HO's lane, SOP/ORG-036):** (1) DEFAULT sub-dispatch = **Agent-tool subagent spawn**, not persistent team-member round-trips — completion rides the reliable tool-return/task-notification (orchestrator self-spawns per ORG-036, CC 2.1.178+); (2) for multi-turn (plan→approve→implement), use `run_in_background` Agent and let the **PARENT drive each round** (parent→child resume reliable, child→parent completion reliable; parent is the locus of control) — this is the exact pattern the T-1316/T-1325 HO-direct arcs ran; (3) reserve peer→idle-peer `SendMessage` for genuine long-lived bidirectional peers only, and treat it as **best-effort** (the active-team smell-test + outside→session status-check is the standing mitigation); (4) the ORG-026 mailbox is NOT the sub-tier fix (persona-scoped + poll-based). Together with the parent watchdog (below) and T-1318, this FULLY covers sub-tier liveness — leaving Layer-3 to backstop only the top-tier persona dispatcher (the T-1319 scope).
- **Sub-tier self-wakeup (OQ-1; T-1318 — RESOLVED 2026-06-26, definitively NO).** Sub-tier agents (project-orchestrator + every tier below) **cannot** arm their own self-wakeup — confirmed three independent ways (Recovery, T-1318): (1) **structural/decisive** — a team-spawned subagent's tool inventory EXCLUDES `ToolSearch` (`bugs/teamcreate-orchestrator-restricted-tool-inventory.md`, re-verified CC 2.1.178+ under T-1291), and `ScheduleWakeup`/`CronCreate` are DEFERRED tools reachable only via `ToolSearch`, with no per-spawn tool-grant override (T-0217) — so a sub-tier agent literally cannot load, let alone call, a self-wakeup primitive; it is structurally impossible, not merely unused; (2) **docs** — subagents run request-response in their own context and cease after returning a summary; idle members re-activate ONLY on an inbound signal (no documented self-wakeup primitive); (3) **live** — plan-first implementers went idle after sending their plan; the b36 (5h27m) and echo-arc-w24 hangs. **Consequence — Layer 1 is NOT uniform per tier:** sub-tier liveness leans on (a) the **parent watchdog** whose ground-truth spans the whole arc (the lead is the locus of all re-activation) + (b) a reliable **parent-facing completion signal**. The `TeammateIdle` hook (exit 2 = keep teammate working) is a parent/config-driven member-tier liveness lever but is still externally driven — it does not change the no-self-wake conclusion. **T-1320 corollary (also confirmed):** the reliable completion path is **task-notification / tool-return to the SPAWNER** (Agent-tool subagent return is rock-solid; task-status can lag per docs); member→member `SendMessage` is the flaky path — restructure sub-tier completion onto spawner-facing signals, not member→member.

## Self-spawning intermediate orchestrators (ORG-036 posture) — the relay contract

*(Added 2026-07-14, sweep T-1769. The June "resolved-operationally" status on the completion-misroute bug did not hold: ORG-036 made self-spawning POs the default, and the mechanism recurred in every arc since — 11+ occurrences, zero exceptions, four cross-linked entries anchored at [`bugs/teammate-orchestrator-agent-dispatch-completion-routes-to-main.md`](../../bugs/teammate-orchestrator-agent-dispatch-completion-routes-to-main.md). This section is the standing contract that replaces it.)*

When the spawner is NOT the session-root (any teammate-orchestrator spawning its own Agent-children), assume as topology, not flake:

1. **Every child stop lands at session-root.** Plan-first turns, guard halts, bubble-ups, completions — all of them notify HO/main, never the spawning orchestrator. The orchestrator's belief "my workers report to me on completion" is false in every observed session.
2. **Session-root relays, promptly and by reference.** A completion landing at root that belongs to an orchestrator's lane gets forwarded the same turn: the essentials + the task output-file path (`/tmp/claude-<uid>/<cwd-slug>/<session>/tasks/<task-id>.output` — teammate-readable; D10: reference, don't re-paste). If the item was HO-gated anyway (plan ruling, bubble-up), root may rule directly and forward the ruling with an explicit routing heads-up.
3. **Orchestrator resumes its own worker by agentId.** Parent→child SendMessage to the stopped worker's raw agentId reliably resumes it with context intact (validated 07-12, 07-14). NEVER re-dispatch a fresh worker just because its report surfaced upstairs — the stopped worker's plan and file-reads survive.
4. **Relay is primary; artifact-poll is the fallback — not the reverse.** The child-stop notification reliably reaches session-root (that leg has never dropped), so the orchestrator's first posture is *wait for the relay*. Artifact-state polling (worktree `git log`/`git status`, `ps` for build processes — NOT transcript-reading) is for the residual case: root-tier wake itself dropped, or the orchestrator has been idle past its own role-threshold with no relay. This reconciles the two July bug entries' apparent poll-vs-don't-poll conflict: don't poll *instead of* the relay contract; do poll *when the relay is overdue*.
5. **Worker briefs carry the two blindness clauses** (from [`bugs/subagent-cannot-identify-dispatcher-messages-or-reply.md`](../../bugs/subagent-cannot-identify-dispatcher-messages-or-reply.md)): mid-task SendMessage directives from `<orchestrator-name>` are from YOUR DISPATCHER — binding task context, not peer traffic (permission-escalation/destructive asks stay refusable per harness injection rules); and you cannot reply — comply or note reasoned non-compliance in your final report. Dispatcher-side corollary: verify directive uptake by artifact, never by assumed ack.
6. **Budget the relay tax into the arc.** Each plan-first checkpoint costs a root round-trip. Root-side: expect ALL of a PO's worker stops on your notification stream and treat forwarding as part of holding the dispatch, per the Layer-2 dispatcher half.
